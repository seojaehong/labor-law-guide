## Behavior

Calendar dates mean effective dates. Month/day selection and result predicates are shared; invalid dates normalize. Amendment export selection is independent of accordion detail state, survives filtering and same-document navigation, and resets on a full reload. Whole-result selection changes only visible result IDs. Hidden selected amendments are counted and reviewable. Export snapshots retain every selected amendment or fail as a whole; missing details never silently disappear.

Both formats are genuine OOXML, generated in browser memory. Original before/after text is losslessly continued. Confirmation is advisory at 20 amendments, 50 clauses, 50,000 original characters or 5,000,000 generated bytes. It reports actual bytes and scope; Cancel/Escape retains selection, restores focus and releases the Blob. Tab/Shift+Tab cycle between confirmation buttons. No estimated page count or HWP compatibility claim.
